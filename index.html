Teeth Cleaning & Exams in Mississauga

Professional teeth cleaning starts at our hygiene suite, where a registered hygienist removes plaque and hardened tartar your toothbrush can't reach, polishes every surface and finishes with fluoride protection. Your cleaning is paired with a digital exam so our Britannia dentist can spot early decay, gum disease and oral changes while they're still simple — and inexpensive — to treat. We tailor recall visits every six to nine months to your risk profile, and welcome every age, from a child's first visit to lifelong maintenance at our Britannia Road West office.

  • 60-minute hygiene visits with gentle ultrasonic scaling
  • Digital low-dose X-rays & oral cancer screening
  • Personalized home-care plan after every visit

Clear Aligners — Invisible Straightening

Clear aligners offer a discreet, removable way to straighten teeth without metal brackets. At our Mississauga clinic we map your smile digitally, then guide you through custom aligners that shift teeth gradually — typically over six to eighteen months. Because they pop out for meals and brushing, aligners fit real life: school, work, weddings and photos. Our team monitors progress at short check-ins, and many patients across Mississauga, Streetsville and Erin Mills choose aligners for mild-to-moderate crowding, spacing and relapse after braces.

  • Virtually invisible, BPA-free aligner trays
  • Digital smile preview before you begin
  • Free aligner consultation with your Britannia dentist

Tooth-Coloured Dental Fillings

Composite fillings let us treat decay and repair chips in a single comfortable visit. After numbing the area, we remove only the damaged structure, then layer and cure a composite matched precisely to your enamel — so modern fillings look like they grew there. We also safely replace old metal amalgam fillings when they crack, leak or simply don't suit your smile anymore. Early fillings protect the tooth's nerve, which is why our dentists on Britannia recommend acting on small cavities quickly rather than watching them grow.

  • Metal-free, shade-matched composite resin
  • Same-visit treatment with gentle local anesthetic
  • Bite adjustment & polishing always included

Full, Partial & Implant Dentures

Whether you need a full denture, a partial to fill a few gaps, or implant-supported dentures that never slip, our Britannia dental centre designs prosthetics around your face, bite and budget. We take precise impressions, select natural-looking tooth shapes and shades with you, and schedule fittings until retention and comfort feel right. Already wear dentures? We handle relines, repairs and adjustments promptly. Modern dentures restore confident chewing, clear speech and facial support — and we walk you through care and routines at every step.

  • Full, partial & implant-supported options
  • Natural-looking acrylic & precision attachments
  • Reline, repair & adjustment services on site

Gentle Tooth Extractions & Wisdom Teeth

Some teeth can't be saved — and removing them gently protects the rest of your smile. Our Mississauga dentists perform simple and surgical extractions, including stubborn wisdom teeth, using precise technique, local anesthetic and optional sedation so the visit is calm from start to finish. You'll leave with clear aftercare instructions and a direct line for questions. Whenever a tooth is removed, we always discuss replacement options — implant, bridge or partial — so your bite, bone and neighbouring teeth stay healthy long after healing.

  • Wisdom tooth & surgical extractions
  • Sedation options for anxious patients
  • Post-op check & replacement planning included

Dental Implants — A Permanent Answer

Dental implants are the closest modern dentistry comes to natural teeth: a titanium root placed in the jaw, topped with a custom crown that looks, feels and chews like your own. Using 3D imaging, our team plans placement around your bone and neighbouring teeth for predictable, lasting results. Implants prevent the bone loss that follows tooth loss, never need special tools to clean, and can support single crowns, bridges or denture anchors. Book a consultation at our Mississauga implant clinic to review timing, health factors and transparent costs.

  • 3D-guided surgical planning
  • Single crowns to full-arch solutions
  • 95%+ long-term success with proper care

Dental Braces for Teens & Adults

For complex crowding, rotated teeth and bite corrections, braces remain the gold standard. Our Britannia dental team offers classic metal braces and tooth-coloured ceramic braces, applying steady, controlled pressure to move teeth exactly where they belong. Adjustments happen every four to eight weeks, and most teen and adult cases finish in eighteen to twenty-four months. We'll honestly compare braces with clear aligners — sometimes the two work together — so you invest in the option that truly fits your case, schedule and budget.

  • Metal & ceramic bracket options
  • Bite (occlusion) correction for all ages
  • Retainers & post-treatment monitoring

Root Canal Therapy That Saves Teeth

A toothache that keeps you up at night often means infection has reached the nerve — and root canal therapy is how we save the tooth. Under comfortable local anesthetic, our dentist removes the inflamed pulp, cleans and disinfects the canals with modern rotary instruments, then seals the tooth, usually restoring it with a crown for long-term strength. Most patients feel relief almost immediately because the source of pain is gone. Done well, a root-canaled tooth can serve you for decades, avoiding extraction and replacement entirely.

  • Modern rotary endodontics & digital imaging
  • Pain relief — not pain — is the goal
  • Crown restoration planned in the same visit
